The pattern: expiry-day options melt every minute the market sits still. The big days are real, but the quiet days pay for them. Morning momentum made +Rs 1,43,199 in 2022 and 2023 at Rs 10,000 a trade, then lost Rs 2,11,196 from 2024.

How the test worked

  • Written down first: the rules, costs and pass mark were fixed in a pre-registration before any code ran or any number was seen.
  • Data: 5-minute Nifty weekly option prices for the contract expiring that day, with each bar's spot and strike. Every Nifty weekly expiry from 2022-01-01 to 2026-07-31: 239 days in all, and each rule skipped the one day a strike it needed was missing, so every rule has 238 trades. Nothing newer than 3 months.
  • Strikes: step 50. At the money = the strike nearest the spot at the entry bar. Prices are bar closes.
  • Fills and costs: buys fill at the bar close plus 1%, sells at the bar close minus 1% (spread and slippage on fast expiry options). Charges per trade: Rs 20 brokerage per order, STT 0.1% of the sell premium, exchange charges 0.035% of premium both sides, GST 18% on brokerage and exchange charges, stamp duty 0.003% of the buy premium, SEBI fee Rs 10 per crore.
  • Results are return on the premium paid, so the changing lot size does not matter. Money examples: Rs 10,000 risked per trade, not compounded.
  • Pass mark: a rule works only if its average net return per trade is above zero in BOTH halves (2022 to 2023, and 2024 to July 2026) AND over the whole period. One positive half is "not reliable".

The 4 rules, exactly

1. Morning momentum

At the 09:30 bar: if Nifty is above the 09:15 bar's open, buy the at-the-money call, else the at-the-money put. Hold to the 15:15 bar close.

2. Morning momentum with a 30% stop

Same entry as rule 1. Exit at the first bar close at or below 70% of the entry price (a 30% stop) or at or above 200% (doubled), else at the 15:15 close.

3. The afternoon lottery ticket

At the 13:00 bar: buy the strike 1% of spot out of the money (rounded to the 50-point step) in the direction of the day so far (Nifty above the 09:15 open: call, else put). Hold to the 15:15 close.

4. Buy both sides (straddle)

At the 09:20 bar: buy the at-the-money call and the at-the-money put. Hold both to the 15:15 close.
